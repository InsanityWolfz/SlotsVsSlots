# Charm and relic ideas (2026-10-04)

The pitch list for the content round. Every idea is automatic (fights are watch-only), takes no decision hints, and
lives on cells (charms) or in the relic bar. The playtester prototypes and measures each one (`playtest/CONTENT_13.md`)
before anything is built.

## Where the roster has gaps
- **Charms are 8, and 3 of them are machine-locked.** Every general charm is a damage/heal number on a sword, a shield
  or a bolt. Nothing touches chips, enemy cheats, odds, or time.
- **VAMP is still the auto-pick** (only-VAMP 16 vs baseline 10); KEEN is the weakest (8.7).
- **JOKER and MIDAS** have no charm of their own (TRICK and INGOT were held in round 12).
- **Relics:** a lot of "pays ×N" and heals. Little against enemy cheats or turtling, and nothing rewards a varied build.

## Charms (each on a cell; levels on the type, cap 3)
| Name | Fits | Rule (LV1/2/3) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LUCRE** | sword, shield, bolt | When its group pays: +1 chip (max 3 / 4 / 5 a fight) | An economy charm: chips feed the shop, bets and the House shield. A new axis. |
| **SAGE** | shield | When it lands: washes 1 / 1 / 2 cheats (slime, rock, hex, bomb) off your reels | Answers the enemies that write on your machine. Pairs with HOLY WATER. |
| **ECHO** | sword | Its group's hit lands again at the start of your next turn for 30 / 40 / 50% | Delayed damage that shows on screen. A different shape from KEEN's flat +N. |
| **WEIGHTED** | any | The cell counts twice on its strip (it lands twice as often); LV2/3 also +10 / +20 | A slot-machine charm: it changes the odds, not the pay. |
| **TRICK** (JOKER only) | sword, shield | When it lands: +10 / 15 / 20 to your jackpot meter | JOKER's own charm, re-measured against THORNY's bar. |
| **KEEN rework** (option) | sword | +30 / 40 / 50 per sword (from 20 / 30 / 40), or pierce + ignore half the enemy's shield next turn | KEEN is a trap at 8.7. |

## Relics
| Name | Tier | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CHARM BRACELET** | uncommon | Everything pays +10% for each different charm type you own | Rewards a varied build; the counter to stacking only VAMP. |
| **METRONOME** | common | Every 3rd spin pays ×2 | Predictable and readable: the crowd counts along. |
| **COUNTERWEIGHT** | common | A spin that pays nothing makes your next spin pay ×1.5 | Softens dead spins. |
| **RUST** | uncommon | The enemy's leftover shield loses 25% each turn | Against turtles (shield-heavy enemies, the Mirror's shields). |
| **SNAKE EYES** | common | Each enemy jackpot heals you 20 | Turns their best moment into a little of yours. |
| **PIT BOSS** | legendary | Once per fight, the enemy's first jackpot pays as a pair | A safety relic for the boss fights. |
| **TESLA COIL** | TESLA | Bolts that don't pay still charge your lightning (5 each) | TESLA's dead bolts do something. |
| **TAX MAN** | MIDAS | Each gold bar group that pays: +1 chip (max 3 a fight) | MIDAS's chip identity, in the relic bar. |

## Gates (the bar to ship)
- A charm's "only X" row sits between baseline and VAMP (it's worth taking, and it's not the new auto-pick).
- A relic's "+X" row is in the 12–15 band (baseline ~10).
- The official table stays inside the gates (WHITE 41–45, GREEN 16–19); no machine moves more than ~2 points.
- Fights stay readable: a new popup or tag at most, no new HUD widgets.
